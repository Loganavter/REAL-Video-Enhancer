Measured 2026-11-17 by timing each phase of `Int8Probe.probe` on the same clip and interpreter, with a
`torch.cuda.synchronize()` after each. The first version of this measurement ran the depth-2 control
over every pair; the shipped loop runs it over the pairs the gate would reuse (4 of 8 test pairs here,
12 forwards), so that row is 1.93 s, not 7.52 s, and the total is 45.1 s, not 50.9 s.

| phase | s | share |
|---|---|---|
| decode (`_read_pairs`, 16 triples by ffmpeg seek) | 2.88 | 6.4% |
| model load and upload | 0.14 | 0.3% |
| the depth-2 control's extra pass (12 fp16 calls) | 1.93 | 4.3% |
| calibrate (24 frames, a hook per conv and per PReLU) | 21.14 | 46.8% |
| forwards (48 fp16 + 24 `_quantised_forward`) | 19.06 | 42.2% |
| **total** | **45.14** | |

Decode is not the cost. Calibration and the forwards are, so a 32-triple sample roughly doubles 89% of
the probe and moves the break-even from 16.2 to about 30 min of video. The place to buy it back is
calibration, where the hooks cost 722 ms a frame - see `hook-cost.txt` for what that is and is not.
