Lowering the threshold shrinks the pair set and leaves the worst value untouched, on both clips. The
pair that carries the damage has a delta below 3.0 - it is a pair the gate *certifies*, at every
threshold in its clamp. So the render-side threshold fix, which removes 12 of the 18 bad frames for
+2.7% of the wall, does not unblock int8 on Chainsaw: the probe reads 0.2177% before and after.

The probe's own filter constant is a separate defect, worth fixing on its own terms: it uses 5.0 while
the auto tuner clamps its choice to (2.0, 4.0), so the probe certifies on a superset of the pairs the
shipped gate can serve. It happens not to change the worst here - the worst pair passes all three - but
the population is not the gate's.

What this leaves: the pairs that carry the damage are inside the certificate, so no threshold on the
gate's own variable can remove them. The lever is the statistic (the difference is a clipped highlight
whose error lives in one saturated channel) or a certificate taken on the rendered output.
