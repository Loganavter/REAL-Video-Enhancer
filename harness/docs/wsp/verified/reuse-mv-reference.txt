# The codec's motion field, resolved and measured where the reuse fails: it does not fix them

Measured 2026-11-20 (local), CPU prototype: PyAV decode with motion-vector side data, `cv2.remap` warps, the
app's own gated/ungated renders of the two labelled clips (`w1040`, `w960`). Records:
`records/reuse-mv-reference.jsonl`; instrument: `harness/scripts/reuse_mv_gate.py` (the reference search and the region
split), `harness/scripts/reuse_compose.py` (the flow route), `harness/scripts/reuse_split.py` (the shipped correction).

This closes the geometry axis of the reuse lane. The queue row is
`rve-reuse-mv-reference-2026-11-20`, and its falsifier is met: the coded field, applied at any reference and
gated per frame, does not take the held-out clip below 3/124.

## What the side data actually says

PyAV surfaces motion vectors only when the decoder option is set **before** decoding:

```python
stream.codec_context.options = {"flags2": "+export_mvs"}
```

Without it frames carry timecode only, and `ffprobe` prints the side-data type but not its contents. The field is
`AVMotionVector`: `dst_x/dst_y` is the block's position in this frame, `src_x/src_y` its position in the
reference, `motion_x/motion_y` the displacement in `motion_scale` units (quarter-pel on H.264), and `source` a
**reference-list tag** (-1 = list0, +1 = list1), not a frame distance.

That tag is what RU035 tripped on. The residual search over the last four display-order frames puts the match at
**two frames back** - the previous P anchor, since this stream's anchors are two display frames apart
(`P B P B`):

| distance | median residual gain | frames improved |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **-16.1%** | 18% |
| **2** | **+2.9%** | **53%** |
| 3 | -0.1% | 49% |
| 4 | -0.6% | 44% |

So the vectors are not wrong; they were applied to the wrong picture. The magnitude matters too: an earlier probe
on the first 18 P frames read +9.6%, which is a prefix of the population. Over all 72 P frames of the cut the
field buys 2.9% - the same population trap this lane has already retracted twice, caught here before it was
recorded.

## The frames that fail, at the distance the gate actually holds

119 of the 124 reused frames of the held-out cut carry a usable field. The four that sit over the bar under the
split correction, in the border band, at the frame's own reuse depth and at the codec's anchor:

| frame | depth | type | gain in the band, reuse depth | gain in the band, distance 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 18 | 3 | P | +3.3% | +1.9% |
| 21 | 2 | B | -10.4% | -10.4% |
| 22 | 3 | P | -5.8% | -16.5% |
| 56 | 1 | P | -32.5% | -24.4% |

Negative on three of four at the depth the gate holds, and negative on three of four at the reference the field
was coded against. The gate holds exactly one HR frame - the last it rendered - so the warp can only be applied at
the reuse depth; the codec's own reference is a different frame in three of these four cases, and the field does
not help there either.

## Where the failures are: a moving border band, measured against a moving target

Splitting each failing frame's pixels-above-the-bar into an 8 px border band (2.3% of the area) and the interior:

| frame | band | interior |
|---|---|---|
| 18 | 9.05% | **0.0000%** |
| 21 | 9.38% | **0.0000%** |
| 22 | 16.67% | **0.0000%** |
| 56 | 0.001% | **0.168%** |

Three of the four are the band, one is not. And the band is not a renderer artefact: its LR content changes 3.2x
faster than the interior's over the segment (1.0x at frame 9, 9.4x at frame 22), and the SR change follows it.
The interior is a held cel; the band is where this clip's picture moves.

The control that fixes what the bar can mean there: two **consecutive freshly rendered** frames of the ungated
render - no reuse involved - differ by more than 8 levels on 14.32% of the band at the worst frame, i.e. 0.33% of
the whole frame, 3.3x the bar. Median over the population: 0.0888%. So a verbatim reuse of any frame fails the
bar on that frame, and the correction's job is to beat 0.33%; it reads 0.19% instead. That is an improvement of
the band and not a pass.

## The other handles, measured and refused

- **A flow from the frame the gate holds** (`reuse_compose.py`: DIS, forward-backward gated, warped SR plus warped
  residual, grain half on top) reads 4/124 on the same clip with the same four frames over the bar and frame 56
  worse (0.150% -> 0.205%), texture 1.238. The second, independent geometry route fails on the same frames.
- **Bilinear residual upsampling** (NEMO upsamples the codec residual) reads 4 over the bar where nearest-2x reads
  3 on the same 12 frames. An edge that has to move is not helped by smoothing.
- **Amplifying the residual at edges** (the correction at gain 2.0 on the frames the mask leaves at unit gain)
  takes frame 56 from 0.166% to 2.775%. The residual under-shoots there because it is a 1080p quantity, not
  because it is too small: scaling it misplaces the 4K edge further.

## What closes, and what ships

The falsifier is met, so the geometry axis is closed: no motion field available to the gate - the codec's, which
references a different picture, or an estimated one from the held frame - reduces the residual where the reuse
fails, and the region that fails is where the picture moves faster than a 1080p residual can express at 4K.

What ships is the measured state, unchanged: **frequency split + recursive correction**, `0/130` and texture 1.001
on the calibration clip, `3/124` and texture 1.021 on the held-out one, 2.428 ms/frame on the GPU. The gate stays
off by default, because three frames out of 124 on action footage carry a visible defect and no cheap statistic
separates them: the failing frames' worst LR delta is 17-62 levels against 54 on passing frames, and frame 56's LR
share above 8 levels is 0.04% against 0.15% on passing ones. Predicting those three would require predicting what
the network does to the 4K detail from the 1080p frame, which is the network itself.

## Reproduce

```bash
# the gate's own decision and the pair of renders (see reuse-decision-statistic.txt for the full recipe)
python3 backend/rve-backend.py ... --input cut.mkv --output on.mkv  --temporal_reuse
python3 backend/rve-backend.py ... --input cut.mkv --output off.mkv
# the reference search and the region split, per reused frame
python3 harness/scripts/reuse_mv_gate.py --clip cut.mkv --depth-log gate.log --emit mv_gate.json
# the flow route from the served reference
python3 harness/scripts/reuse_compose.py --gated on.mkv --ungated off.mkv --lr cut.mkv --depth-log gate.log \
    --alpha 1.35 --fb-tol 1.0 --emit compose.json
# the shipped correction
python3 harness/scripts/reuse_split.py --gated on.mkv --ungated off.mkv --lr cut.mkv --depth-log gate.log \
    --alpha 1.35 --recursive --emit split.json
```

## NOT checked

Bi-predicted blocks carry two vectors for one block and are used here as two separate fields, not weighted as
the codec weights them; occlusion is not detected (a vector with no matching source content shows up as a
negative gain and nothing else); no GPU implementation of the warp was written, so the geometry route's cost is
unmeasured beyond the 2.428 ms of the correction it would replace; and the 4K grain spectrum of the added
residual is still unmeasured.
