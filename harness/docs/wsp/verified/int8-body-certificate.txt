# The first-frame gate stays - as a gross sanity check, with its population on the line

No new measurement. This file records a decision about an instrument, and it is written because the log
line it replaces printed a percentage that no population supported: on every int8 run of 2026-11-18 the
body printed `self-certification on the first frame: 0.00000% (bar 0.1%) -> serving`, while the whole clip
on the same material reads 0.53% body-against-engine and 356 of 494 frames above the bar.

## What the gate is actually for

`Int8Body.certify` compares this body against the fp16 model on one frame, and it exists to catch a
coverage bug - a grid that does not cover the activations.

**Where the numbers come from, stated because one of them is not a record.** The signature quoted in the
kernel's docstring - **3.53%** at 1920x1080 against 0.00004% for the validated pipeline, 0.00000% at
256x256, 1.23% at 512x512, "an error that grows with resolution" - was written when that bug was live and
is NOT in `harness/docs/wsp/verified/`: it is a docstring figure. The record holds the opposite state of the same
kernel: body against the MODEL at 1080p reads **0.00442-0.01917%** over seven frames
(`int8-scales-bd.txt`, the body-vs-model table), i.e. 180x below the docstring's 3.53%. Either the coverage
bug was fixed after that docstring was written, or the 3.53% was measured on a different (broken)
configuration; nothing here separates the two, and no one has recorded body-vs-model at any resolution
other than 1080p. Two broken correction experiments read **29.5%** and **100%** and were refused
(`int8-scales-bd.txt`) - those two numbers ARE in the record, and they are what makes a gross gate a real
gate rather than decoration.

What it cannot do is certify the picture. The acceptance statistic is `bar_share` = 0.1% of pixels on the
**worst frame of a spread sample** (n>=2, `harness/docs/claims-bars.json`); one frame has no population, and on
this material the number is dominated by which frame the clip opens on. Borrowing the acceptance bar also
risked the failure class this project already paid for once: a correct body refusing on a hard first frame.

## What changed

| before | after |
|---|---|
| gate at the acceptance bar, 0.1% | gate at **1.0%** (`FIRST_FRAME_SANITY`), a gross-error threshold |
| `self-certification on the first frame: X% (bar 0.1%) -> serving` | `first-frame sanity: X% (n=1, gross gate 1.0%; NOT an acceptance number - the picture is judged on renders)` |
| refusal message said only "refusing" | names the signature: "the quantiser's grid does not cover this frame at this resolution" |

The threshold sits between the two clusters: the worst **valid** reading on record is 0.28825% (a
deliberately broken grid margin x1.5, `int8-scales-bd.txt`), and the smallest **bug** reading is the
docstring's 3.53% - so 1.0% gives ~3.5x margin either way, with the smaller cluster's edge on the record and
the larger one's on the docstring. `Int8Body.sanity_ok(share, bar)` is a pure function so the separation is pinned
without a GPU, and `harness/tests/test_int8_probe_modes.py` asserts both clusters (0.00000, 0.00442,
0.01917, 0.28825 pass; 3.53, 29.5, 100 fail).

Evidence it runs: BD Violet, int8 forced, reuse off, 2026-11-18
(`harness/docs/wsp/verified/logs/sanity/bd_violet20s_int8_r1_null.log`) -

```
int8 body first-frame sanity: 0.00007% of pixels >8 levels against the fp16 model
(n=1, gross gate 1.0%; NOT an acceptance number - the picture is judged on renders)
```

## The resolution envelope, in code

- **Accuracy of the body: measured at LR 1920x1080 only** - 0.00442-0.01917% against the model on seven
  frames. Nothing on record covers 256x256, 512x512 or any other size, so the docstring's resolution
  sweep cannot be confirmed or refuted from the record.
- **The app refuses the body above 2.5 Mpx of LR input**
  (`UpscaleTorch`: `pixels > 2_500_000` -> refuse, logged as "measured 0.77x at 4K against 0.96x at
  1080p"). LR 1080p is 2.07 Mpx and fits; LR 1440p is 3.69 Mpx and does not. That refusal is a SPEED
  decision, not the gate above.
- Two further conditions, both checked in code: the model needs 64 body channels (`Int8Body` raises, the
  render logs "int8: build failed" and the fp16 engine runs), and the body serves the whole-frame path
  only (with the temporal gate on, the render is tiled and the body is not wired in).

## Not covered

- The gate is NOT re-validated against a broken build here: 29.5% and 100% are read off the record, 3.53%
  off a docstring. Reproducing a coverage bug is a separate job and would have to change the kernel on
  purpose - and until someone does, the lower edge of "broken" is a docstring figure.
- One frame is still one frame. A body that is subtly wrong on this clip's opening and right elsewhere
  passes; that is the accepted price of a gross gate, and the picture's real judge is the render pair.
- The certificate the probe used to take (the composition statistic, `certify=False` since 2026-11-19) is
  a different object and stays withdrawn; this file is only about the body's own first-frame gate.

## Reproduce

```bash
CLIPS=bd_violet20s.mkv ROUNDS=1 SINK=null LOGDIR=/tmp/x harness/scripts/wall_ab.sh
grep -a "first-frame sanity" /tmp/x/*int8*.log
cd backend && PYTHONPATH=../scripts QT_QPA_PLATFORM=offscreen <install-python> -m pytest tests/test_int8_probe_modes.py -q
```
