## The bound, not the anecdote

Markov on one block of 1024 pixels: if the block's mean absolute deviation is `tau`, the number of
pixels above 8 levels is at most `1024*tau/8`, so the share is at most `tau/8`. With the gate's own
3.29 that bound is **41%** - the certificate permits a share 400 times the bar. To certify 0.1% by this
statistic the block mean would have to be **0.008 levels**, which is 500x below the threshold of 4.0
and 412x below what frame 164 actually read.

So the mean cannot be tightened into safety; it has to be replaced by the quantity the bar measures.

## Three substitutions, not one

The gate decides on a block mean of **luma** at **low resolution**; the bar counts pixels of
**channel-max** error at **full resolution**. The ledger has already measured the third one alone:
channel-max against luma differs 8x (0.29% against 0.036%) on the reuse artifact, which means a
chromatic change on a static frame is invisible to this gate without any motion at all. The second is
the model's amplification, and is not measured here.

## What is already the better half of the choice

The gate runs `mean_abs` (`box(|I - ref|)`), not `block_mean` (`|box(I) - box(ref)|`), and the module's
own note measures that difference on the moving fist: 0.004% against 0.460% at threshold 8, at the same
70.2% reuse. So "switch to the other statistic" is not the fix - the better of the two is already
shipping, and it still cannot certify a per-pixel count.
