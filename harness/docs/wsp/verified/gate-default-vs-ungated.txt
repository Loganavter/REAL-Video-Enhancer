# The shipped fp16 gate exceeds the acceptance bar on Chainsaw, and its own certificate says it does not

Measured 2026-11-17 by rendering the same clip twice through `backend/rve-backend.py` with the bundled
interpreter (torch 2.9.0+cu130), one GPU job at a time: once with the shipped defaults (auto gate on,
`threshold 4.0`, chain 3, 100/481 frames reused) and once with `--no_temporal_reuse`. Everything else -
engine, decoder, model, resolution - is identical.

This is the control `int8-d2-owner-split` left open. The probe's depth-2 number (0.139-0.191% on
Chainsaw) is not the render's population: the probe renders the earliest frame of a decoded triple
against later references, uses torch fp16 instead of the TensorRT plan, and approximates the gate's
served reference with block-mean steps. Any of those three could read 0.19% where the render reads
0.02%. This control has none of them.

| | |
|---|---|
| worst frame (164) | **0.49372%** |
| frames above the 0.1% bar | **18 of 481** |
| peak on the worst frame | 14 levels |
| A/A - the gated render twice | 0.00000%, 481/481 decoded frames identical |
| the gate's own certificate (its log) | p90 d1 0.73 / d2 0.69 / d3 0.66 against limits 2 / 12 / 12 |

Only reused frames can differ: the render is deterministic (the A/A is exact), so every frame the gate
recomputed is identical, and all 18 frames above the bar are among the 100 it reused (20.8%). The
difference is the gate, and nothing else.

**So the answer to the fork is the first outcome.** The default fp16 configuration exceeds the project's
own acceptance statistic on this clip. int8 is not the blocker here; the gate's certification is.

Both numbers are true, and that is the finding: the gate certifies a p90 of the block-mean delta on the
low-resolution input (limit 12), while the acceptance statistic counts high-resolution pixels more than
8 levels off. A mean over a 32x32 block does not bound a per-pixel count - on the worst frame the HR
block-mean reads 3.91 while 0.49% of the pixels are off by more than 8. This is E25's gap, measured for
the first time on the product's own output rather than on a proxy.

## What this does not cover

- **One clip.** Chainsaw 20 s is the title where reuse is lowest (20.8%) and motion highest. Violet and
  the smoke clip are not measured this way, and the result must not be carried to them.
- **The bar is the int8 lane's picture criterion.** The gate was never certified against it, so this is
  the first measurement of the gate on that statistic, not a regression. Whether 0.1% is the right bar
  for a gate that reuses a frame a viewer never sees re-rendered is an owner decision.
- **The container is not the shipped one.** The renders were written lossless (`ffv1` / `yuv444p`) so
  the encoder stays out of the comparison; the default is libx264 crf 18. The container round trip
  reads max 2 levels and 0.000000% above 8 on a synthetic frame, and the A/A is exact.
- **"Gated vs ungated" is a picture difference, not a visibility judgement.** The 18 frames are the ones
  to look at; whether they are noticeable is not decided here.
