## The 6.3-level peak is chroma, not rounding

RGB -> yuv420p -> RGB on one 4K frame: **peak 24, share 0.1071%, mean 1.77**. So an engine-RGB output
against a YUV-stored render carries a representation term three times the bar's 8 levels, and "rounding"
was the wrong label. Inside the probe both sides are RGB (torch against engine), so the term is not in
the composition number - it is in any comparison of the engine against the encoded render.

The engine's own error is **87% of the bar** on Violet: it eats the budget and composition adds the
rest. The probe's own-defect reading (0.082%) was close to this, so that number was right even when the
composition one was not.

## The marginal band is one frame, and it is safe

Around the 0.07 threshold (+-2%), Chainsaw has exactly **one** frame: 475, LR 0.07108, HR 0.07546,
rejected in the simulation and reused in the product. It is also the worst HR among all rejected
frames - under the bar. So the 1.6% simulation/product jitter can flip it either way and the frame
stays inside the bar; no rejected frame hides a failure.

## The second condition is renamed

`--temporal_reuse_block_max` -> `--temporal_reuse_max_block_mean` (and `block_max` ->
`max_block_mean` in the gate), because it is `max of block means`, not `max within block` - which is
exactly why it never fired at 4 or 8 levels.
