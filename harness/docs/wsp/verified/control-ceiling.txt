A maximum over a subset cannot exceed the maximum over the set. `harness/scripts/check_control_ceiling.py`
takes a probe control record and a sweep record and exits 1 when the control is above the sweep.

It fires on both clips:

| clip | probe control | full fp16 sweep | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chainsaw | 0.083% | 0.07546% | **VIOLATION** |
| Violet | 0.077% | 0.07345% | **VIOLATION** |

That is the honest state and not a formality: the probe re-decides which frames the gate reuses, so its
pair set is not a subset of the render's, and its composition number is about a different population
than the one the bar judges. The check is the mechanical form of the rule; making it pass needs the
probe to replay the render's decisions, which it cannot do without the render.

Usage, and the exact output, are in `logs/control-ceiling.log`.

Run: PYTHONPATH=. python3 -m harness.cli check harness/docs/wsp/verified/control-ceiling.txt \
       --records harness/docs/wsp/verified/records/control-ceiling.jsonl --bars harness/docs/claims-bars.json
