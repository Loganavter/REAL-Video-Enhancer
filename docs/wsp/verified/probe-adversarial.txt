# Adversarial pair selection: the probe moves from 26x optimistic to 1.24x conservative

The spread sample can only find damage it happens to land on. On Chainsaw the product's 16 triples
produce **no depth-2 pair at all**, so the verdict rested on depth 1 and read 0.000% where the render
shows 0.08876%.

`probe(..., adversarial=k)` now scans the whole clip with the gate's own channel-max statistic (no model,
one streaming pass), ranks the reuses it would make, and renders the top-k.

| measurement | Chainsaw | Violet |
|---|---|---|
| spread sample (16 triples) | **0.000%** (no depth-2 pair) | 0.167% |
| **adversarial top-13** | **0.110%** (control 0.083%) | **0.168%** (control 0.077%) |
| full render, ground truth | 0.08876% | 0.56613% |

On Chainsaw the number moves from 26x **under** the render to 1.24x **over** it, and the verdict flips
from int8 to fp16 - a refusal of a configuration the render shows passing (0.08876 against a 0.1% bar).
That is the safe direction: the probe now errs by refusing, not by admitting.

On Violet nothing moves: with 70.8% of frames reused, the spread sample already lands on the damage, and
the adversarial scan confirms 0.168%.

## Two bugs the self-check caught

- **`select=eq(n,index)` does not return the stream's frame index** under B-frames. Twelve of thirteen
  ranked pairs came back with a measured share above the gate's threshold (0.18% against the 0.057% the
  scan recorded) - pairs the gate would never serve. Replaced by one streaming pass that counts frames.
- **Without a self-check the bogus pairs rendered a scene change as a composition error**: the first run
  read 86.7%, with an fp16 control of 86.3% - the control is what made it obvious, because the gate's
  own term cannot be 86% on a pair it certified. Every pair is now re-measured from the decoded pixels
  and dropped if its share exceeds the gate's threshold.
