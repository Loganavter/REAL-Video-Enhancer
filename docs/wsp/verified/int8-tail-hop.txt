# The tail is a seventeenth int8 hop: 3.2 ms of the frame, 0.00000% of pixels

Measured 2026-11-20 (local), RTX 4060 Ti, BD Violet 1920x1080, int8 body (head fp16, 16 int8 hops, tail).
Three staged steps, cheapest first: a CPU/GPU emulation, then a kernel on its own, then the app.
Records: `int8-tail-hop.jsonl`; raw: `logs/tail-hop/emulation.json`, `logs/tail-hop/kernel.json`,
`logs/tail-hop/ab/`, `logs/tail-hop/pair.json`.

Row `rve-tail-as-int8-hop-2026-11-19` (researcher's C1). The last hop writes int8 codes on a per-channel
grid; the shipped tail dequantises them to fp16 (a full 400 MB pass) and feeds a cuDNN fp16 convolution.
A convolution is linear in its input, so `w'[k,o] = s_k * w[k,o]` folded offline makes the codes themselves
the input: one kernel instead of two, no dequant pass, the integer accumulation exact (64*9 terms of
127*127 = 9.3e6, far below 2^24).

## Stage (a): the picture, before any kernel

Four held-out BD frames (29, 44, 274, 400), calibrated on the eight spread frames, the corpus's own
emulation recipe:

| frame | int8 tail vs today's tail | algebra only (no weight quantisation) |
|---|---|---|
| 29 | 0.00113%, peak 10 | 0.00096%, peak 10 |
| 44 | 0.00113%, peak 11 | 0.00109%, peak 10 |
| 274 | 0.00147%, peak 11 | 0.00129%, peak 11 |
| 400 | 0.00063%, peak 13 | 0.00051%, peak 13 |
| **worst** | **0.00147% / 13** | **0.00129% / 13** |

Bars are 0.1% and 20 levels, so the worst frame sits **68x inside the share bar**. The fourth column is the
control: the fold's algebra alone reads 0.00129%, so quantising the folded weights to int8 adds
**0.0002%** - the fold is not what costs, and the estimate that it would was worth checking.

## Stage (b): one kernel on its own

| arm | median of rounds 2+ |
|---|---|
| the shipped tail (dequant + cuDNN fp16 conv) | **4.444 ms** |
| dequantise_nhwc alone | 1.839 ms |
| cuDNN fp16 conv alone | 2.556 ms |
| **one int8 Triton conv 576 -> 16 + epilogue** | **0.974 ms** |

**-3.47 ms per frame, 78% of the tail.** Against the recorded in-app conv (2.35 ms) the candidate reads
0.974 ms for conv *and* epilogue, 2.6x the conv alone. On the same codes the kernel's output is within
max 0.007 of the cuDNN tail (mean 0.0002 on an output whose mean is 0.006) - the weight quantisation, as
stage (a) said.

## Stage (c): in the app, and the picture on the full clip

Null sink, one session, arms alternating, 3 rounds:

| round | tail = cuDNN fp16 | tail = one int8 kernel |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 45.87 | 42.62 |
| 2 | 45.83 | 42.71 |
| 3 | 45.93 | 42.71 |
| **median** | **45.87** | **42.71** |

**-3.16 ms per frame (-6.9%)**, in 3 rounds of 3. The body's own phase counter attributes it: the tail line
reads dequant 1.67 -> **0.00**, conv 2.49 -> **0.96**, SUM 5.66 -> **2.56** - the pass is gone and the
convolution is 2.6x faster, while shuffle and residual are unchanged (0.90 -> 0.78 and 0.94 -> 0.98).

Lossless ffv1 renders of both arms, then `gate_sweep --reference-kind config`:

```
494 frames, block 64
worst share 0.00000% (frame 0), frames above 0.1%: 0
worst block mean 0.48 (frame 225), peak 6
```

Invisible to the decision statistic on the whole clip. Two one-frame checks say the same more sharply: on
frames 29 and 274 the int8 tail reads **0.00000% / peak 1-2** against the shipped tail, and against the
MODEL it is *closer* than the cuDNN path on both - 0.01131% -> 0.01057% (peak 17 both) on frame 29 and
0.01613% -> 0.01501% (peak 16 -> 15) on frame 274.

## Adopted

`RVE_TAIL_INT8` defaults to **1** since 2026-11-20; `RVE_TAIL_INT8=0` restores the cuDNN path for an A/B.
Why adopted where the epilogue fold was not: that one bought 0.22 ms and its mechanism was unexplained;
this one buys 3.16 ms, removes a pass outright, and is measurably closer to the model on the frames checked.

The guard: this kernel pads 16 output channels, so a model whose tail has more than 16 is declined with a
warning and served by cuDNN. Nothing here changes the body's own coverage boundary (64 body channels).

## Not covered

- One card, one clip, one label, 2x model. The tail's output count is 12 for this model family; the padding
  argument ("zeros contribute nothing") is what keeps other counts of 16 or fewer admissible, and that is
  arithmetic, not a measurement of them.
- The picture is a config-vs-config pair plus two model-referenced frames, not 494 frames against the model.
- The peak bar's own status is unchanged: `bar_int8_peak` = 20 still carries a PENDING OWNER DECISION note,
  and the peaks quoted here are deltas between two int8 configurations, not the int8 path's deviation from
  the model (that one is 15-17 on the two frames above).
- Timing is null sink, reuse off; the NVENC arm is not measured here.
- Suite: `pytest backend/tests` reads 409 passed, 1 failed - the failure is `ModuleNotFoundError: yaml` in
  `scripts/harness/db.py` under the app's portable interpreter, which has no PyYAML; no file of this change
  is on that import path.
