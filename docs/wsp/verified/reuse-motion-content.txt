# The class the population was missing: dense moving line art, where the correction fails by 30x and the gate refuses

Measured 2026-11-20 (local), the app's own renderer (TensorRT, 2x Futsuu Anime, 1080p -> 4K, ffv1 lossless sink).
Records: `records/reuse-motion-content.jsonl`; clip provenance: `sources.yaml` (`motion8s.mkv`).

The queue row `rve-reuse-motion-content-2026-11-20` asked for the one content class the accepted population does
not cover. Every failure measured so far was either a near-blank flash whose whole error is a 1-2 px border line, or
a still face with a 21/255 line-art softening. Dense line art that *moves* was not in the population, and it is the
class where the residual's error would scale with the moving area instead of hiding in a border.

## How the segment was picked

An automated scan of the whole episode (36 612 frames of the 25:27 BDRemux, decoded at 480p) computed per frame the
share of detail (gradient > 20) and the share of pixels changed by more than 8 levels against the previous frame.
Episode medians: **detail 4.07%, motion 0.48%**. The best 8 s window by their product is **163-171 s** - a
bowling-alley scene, several characters, line art across the frame, the hero throwing a ball - at **detail 15.6%,
motion 40.6%**, i.e. 4x and 85x the episode medians. The cut was made before any acceptance number was looked at.

Provenance is a packet subsequence, not a re-encode: `-c copy`, 204 packets, **38.3 Mbps**, and the first ten packet
sizes of the cut match the source at offset **3910** exactly. Recorded in `sources.yaml`.

## The gate refuses it, and that is the product's protection

With the app's own auto tuner (`--temporal_reuse`), the noise floor reads **31.16** against the **1.5** refusal
limit, and reuse is switched off: **0 of 204 frames**. The same tuner engages on the 1040 s cut (floor 0.87) and on
960 s, so the refusal is content-driven, not a global setting.

@claim RU048 31.16 levels "frames [b28472c139dbce0b] n=204" measured

## Forced through, the correction fails on every frame

Forcing the gate (`--no_temporal_reuse_auto --temporal_reuse_threshold 20`, i.e. blocks allowed to move by up to 20
levels) still reuses only **8 of 204 frames (3.9%)**, all at depth 1 - the picture really does change everywhere.
On those eight the split + recursive correction reads:

**Eight of eight over the bar**, worst 3.19% - 30x the bar and 16x the worst frame of the held-out action clip. The
failure is **interior**, the opposite of the earlier class: 2.78-2.91% of the interior against 0.14-0.48% of the
band, with peaks of **171-189 levels**. The picture is wrong, not softened: at frame 24 the hair strands are doubled
and shifted (`images/motion24_ours.png` against `images/motion24_fresh.png`).

The two numbers that separate this class from the accepted one: amplitude (189 levels against 39) and location
(interior against a 1-2 px border line). The accepted population's worst frames are frames where the picture does
not move; here it moves everywhere, and a 1080p residual cannot move 4K line art.

## What this changes

Nothing in the accepted result, and one thing in the reasoning: **the gate's auto refusal is load-bearing, not
incidental.** The class where the correction is visibly broken is exactly the class where the tuner's noise floor
exceeds its limit and reuse never happens. That is why the product default (gate off, opt-in) and the tuner's own
refusal agree with each other.

It also bounds the verdict `reuse-residual-imperceptible`: the residual is imperceptible on the population it was
judged on - frames that are blank or still - and this cut is the counterexample that shows why the class had to be
measured rather than assumed.

## Reproduce

```bash
# the cut (stream copy; the packet-subsequence proof is in sources.yaml)
ffmpeg -v error -ss 163 -t 8 -i "<BDRemux>" -c copy motion8s.mkv
# the gate's own decision, and the refusal line
RVE_DEBUG_DEPTH=1 python3 backend/rve-backend.py ... --input motion8s.mkv --output null --temporal_reuse
# forced reuse, the pair, and the correction
RVE_DEBUG_DEPTH=1 python3 backend/rve-backend.py ... --input motion8s.mkv --output null \
    --temporal_reuse --no_temporal_reuse_auto --temporal_reuse_threshold 20
python3 backend/rve-backend.py ... --input motion8s.mkv --output on.mkv  --temporal_reuse \
    --no_temporal_reuse_auto --temporal_reuse_threshold 20
python3 backend/rve-backend.py ... --input motion8s.mkv --output off.mkv
python3 scripts/reuse_split.py --gated on.mkv --ungated off.mkv --lr motion8s.mkv \
    --depth-log gate.log --alpha 1.35 --recursive --emit split.json
```

## NOT checked

The band/interior split in `mot_class.json` was recomputed with an OpenCV box filter rather than the
integral-image one `reuse_split.py` uses, so its shares read 0.1-0.3 points lower than the table's (`3.03%` against
`2.89%` at frame 15); the class conclusion - interior, 171-189 levels - does not depend on that difference. The
segment is one 8.5 s cut of one episode, and the forced configuration is a stress test, not a product setting: what
it establishes is that the correction *would* break here, not that the product permits it. No perceptual metric was
run on this class (VMAF is known to be blind to doubled thin lines, and the owner's eye already settled the accepted
population).
