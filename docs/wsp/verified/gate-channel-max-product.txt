## What it does to int8

The probe's pair filter now uses the gate's own variable (it used `mean(R,G,B)` - neither the gate's
`(1,2,1)/4` luma nor the bar's channel-max).

| clip | sample | depth-2 composition | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chainsaw | 16 triples | no depth-2 pairs at all | int8, on depth 1 alone |
| Chainsaw | 32 triples | **0.0034%** (3 pairs) | **int8** |
| Violet | 16 triples | 0.167% (7 pairs) | fp16 |

Under the shipped luma gate the same Chainsaw sample read 0.1748% at depth 2 - a 51x reduction, and the
number now sits 29x under the bar instead of 1.7x over it.

Two things this does not fix:

- **Violet still fails, and on depth 1** (0.108%), where no reuse is involved: that is the quantiser's
  own error on that material, not the gate's. The gate fix does not touch it.
- **The product's default sample finds no depth-2 pairs on Chainsaw** (16 triples = 8 test pairs, 17.3%
  reuse), so its verdict rests on depth 1 alone. A depth-2 certificate needs more triples; the cost of
  that is in `probe-cost.txt`.
