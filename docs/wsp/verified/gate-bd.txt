# The gate lane on BD: it fails where it engages, and it is off by default now

> **CLOSED BY THE OWNER, 2026-11-18.** Frame reuse is shelved, off by default, and **any further
> investigation of it is forbidden** (`rve-reuse-gate-forbidden-2026-11-18`). This file is the record of
> what was measured before that order, not an open question: do not re-run these cells, do not extend
> them, and do not treat the rows below as a road map.

Measured 2026-11-18 on BD stream copies (`sources-bd.txt` proves the cuts are `-c copy`). The owner's
decision, taken on these numbers: **frame skipping is shelved and OFF by default** - on a 1080p source
the gate's own noise floor is almost always above its 1.5 refusal limit, because the material always
carries digital noise, and at low contrast a small delta can be significant motion rather than noise.
`--temporal_reuse` is now the opt-in flag; `--no_temporal_reuse` is a deprecated no-op.

The engine half of the old headline is untouched: 1.63x, every frame, every clip. What follows is the
gate half, which ships nowhere.

2. **Where it refuses, the render is the ungated render to the bit**: Violet 494/494 and the battle cut

## The noise floor rises when the re-encode is replaced by the BD

Same shot, same session, both re-run today:

| clip | 4 Mbps re-encode | BD stream copy | limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Violet same shot | **1.23** (69.8% reuse, chain 4) | **1.67** (refused) | 1.5 |
| Chainsaw calm shot | **0.66** (20.8% reuse, chain 3) | **1.00** (25.6% reuse, chain 4) | 1.5 |

The queue expected the floors to fall ("compression noise was being counted as material noise"). They
rise: a 4 Mbps encode that stays static inside a GOP makes consecutive frames more alike, while the BD
keeps the grain the gate reads as motion. On Violet the limit sits between the two numbers, which is why
the gate engaged on that shot at all.

Both 4 Mbps runs are in this session (the old 1.23 / 0.66 were recorded hours earlier), because this
project has already paid once for a cross-session quotient.

## int8 on BD: two orders of magnitude between two clips

| clip | int8 worst share | frames > 0.1% | what was recorded on the re-encode |
|---|---|---|---|
| Violet BD | **0.53318%** | 356 / 494 | 0.08876%, zero frames (int8-and-violet-sweeps) |
| Chainsaw same-shot BD | **0.00339%** | 0 / 539 | probe 0.010%, peak 19 |

So int8 is not simply "broken on real material": on the calm Chainsaw shot it is nearly exact, and on
Violet it is over the bar on 72% of frames. The difference is the per-tensor activation grids, which are
calibrated per clip - the lane already recorded that calibration must be split per title
(`int8-split-calibration`); what is new is that the outcome spans two orders of magnitude, so "int8 is
accurate" is not a statement about the engine, it is a statement about the clip.

The probe's own numbers move with the configuration as well as the clip - Violet reads engine 0.082% on
7 pairs in an fp16 run and 0.688% on 7 pairs in an int8-forced run, the Chainsaw cuts are probed on 4
and 2 pairs - so probe numbers are comparable only inside one configuration. Queued as
`rve-int8-probe-population-2026-11-18`.

The gate's 1.24x on the calm shot is real and is what it costs 0.52379% of pixels for. int8 is 1.14x on
Violet and slightly SLOWER than fp16 on Chainsaw - the body only serves the whole-frame path, so the win
is a property of the clip as much as of the quantiser.

## What is not covered

- One 20 s cut per shot. The floor is a property of the calibration window, so a different opening can
  read differently; the BD cuts also start 9-11 frames earlier than the re-encodes (a `-c copy` cut lands
  on a keyframe), so the windows are the same shot, not the same frame indices.
- The BD cuts declare no colour matrix and no range (the re-encodes declared `tv`). Inside a
  gated-vs-ungated or int8-vs-fp16 delta that is common-mode; against the source it is not, and it is not
  resolved here.
- The channel-max and int8 cells on the Chainsaw clip were NOT run: they answered "which gate cell should
  ship", and nothing ships now.
- The wall rows are single runs of a 20 s clip, not three interleaved rounds: they are large effects
  (1.14x, 1.24x) but they are one draw each.
