# The head conv is not layout-bound - it is a shape that cannot be made efficient, and the lever is fusion

Measured 2026-11-19, isolated torch conv, 1920x1080, fp16, random tensor (latency does not depend on values),
one GPU job, 10 iterations behind 3 warm-ups. Serialised arm-by-arm inside one process.
Records: `int8-head-conv.jsonl`; raw sweep: `docs/wsp/verified/logs/head-conv/probe.json`.

Why this was measured: the phase counter puts `head + quantise` at 6.25 ms of a 50 ms frame, and the
quantise half (1.58) sits at its own traffic floor (1.38 of 1.58 for 398 MB), which leaves **4.65-5.2 ms for
a 7.17 GFLOP convolution** - 1.5 TFLOPS, 3.5% of this card's fp16 CUDA-core peak. Nothing in the corpus had
ever timed that convolution on its own.

## What it responds to

| input | weights | ms | TFLOPS | x its 0.96 ms traffic floor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NCHW | NCHW | 3.788 | 1.89 | 3.9 |
| NHWC view | NCHW | 3.411 | 2.10 | 3.5 |
| NCHW | channels_last | 3.290 | 2.18 | 3.4 |
| **NHWC view** | **channels_last** | **3.196** | **2.24** | **3.3** |
| NCHW, input padded 3 -> 8 | channels_last | 3.936 | 1.82 | 4.1 |
| NHWC view, input padded 3 -> 8 | channels_last | 3.663 | 1.96 | 3.8 |

**The layout lever that gave the tail 2.5x gives the head 19%** (3.79 -> 3.20). Padding the input to 8
channels - which the engine build does so myelin can fuse the head with its PReLU - makes it *slower* here.
So the row's premise ("the head is layout-bound like the tail") is refuted.

## Why: the shape sits far on the memory side of the roofline

- arithmetic intensity = 7.17 GFLOP / 278 MB = **26 FLOP/byte**, against this card's ridge of ~611. A
  convolution with 3 input channels writes 265 MB to compute 7 GFLOP: it can never be compute-bound.
- K = 27 (3 x 3 x 3) against a 16-wide tensor-core k-step: 27/32 of the tensor-core work is useful at best,
  and 27 is too small to amortise the epilogue over.
- The best variant is still **3.3x its own traffic floor**, so even the write is not being served well - that
  is where the remaining 2.2 ms lives, and no layout knob tested here moves it.

## What that leaves

The head conv's arithmetic is small and its output is large, which makes the pass itself the target:

- **fuse head conv + PReLU + quantise into one kernel that writes int8 codes** (133 MB instead of 265 MB,
  and the 1.58 ms quantise pass disappears). Ceiling: the 265 MB fp16 write at 288 GB/s is 0.92 ms, plus the
  pass; realistically 1.5-2.5 ms of the 6.25. Filed as `rve-head-quantise-fusion-2026-11-19`.
- Everything layout-side is now measured and closed: NCHW/NHWC in either direction, weights in either
  layout, and the 3 -> 8 padding, all within 3.2-4.5 ms of each other.

## Not covered

- The in-app head reads 4.65-5.2 ms against 3.2 isolated. The difference is the clamp, the input conversion
  and whatever the frame pipeline adds; this file does not decompose it.
- One card, one resolution, fp16 only. The convolution is called with `cin=3` because that is what the model
  has; a different model family would need its own sweep.
- The fusion's arithmetic identity is NOT established: a Triton head would compute the conv in its own order,
  so byte-identity is not a given, and the picture bar (0.1% worst frame) is the fallback criterion.
