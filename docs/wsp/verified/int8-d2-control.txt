# The depth-2 blocker has two owners, and the reference fix moved Violet by 37-214%

Measured with the product's own probe (`backend/src/utils/Int8Probe.py`) through
`scripts/probe_int8_app.py`, one GPU job at a time, bundled interpreter torch 2.9.0+cu130.

The probe now prints, on the SAME pairs and at the SAME separation it reads the composition on, the fp16
reference against fp16. Only the renderer changes, so the gap between the two lines is the quantiser's
share of the depth-2 number and the rest is the gate's own reference.

**The reference was wrong in the first version of this file.** `_calibrate` loads int8-rounded weights
into the model, and `ref` was computed after it - so the "fp16" side was fp16 arithmetic on quantised
weights, measured at peak 4 levels and 0.00000% above 8. It was assumed not to move any number. It does:

| clip | sample | d2 before | d2 after | change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chainsaw | 16 | 0.217689% | 0.217737% | +0.02% |
| Chainsaw | 32 | 0.174793% | 0.174805% | +0.01% |
| Violet | 16 | 0.121950% | **0.167450%** | **+37%** |
| Violet | 24 | 0.024800% | **0.038219%** | **+54%** |
| Violet | 32 | 0.026452% | **0.083189%** | **+214%** |

Chainsaw is unmoved because its number is dominated by the reference term; Violet is not, because its
number is dominated by the quantiser, and the quantised reference had been hiding part of it. The
reference is now taken from the original weights before `_calibrate` runs.

## The product's sample: 16 triples, 8 test pairs, 2-6 of them at depth 2

Label convention for every table here: `frames=N` counts decoded TRIPLES, the test pairs are every other
triple (`N/2`), and the depth-2 subset is smaller still. So 16 triples = 8 test pairs, 24 = 12, 32 = 16.

`RenderVideo._probe_int8_fit` calls `probe(...)` with the default `frames=16`.

| clip | d2 int8 | d2 fp16 | fp16 share of d2 | d1 int8 | d1 fp16 | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chainsaw Man 20 s | 0.2177% | **0.1910%** | 88% | 0.000072% | 0.000% | fp16 |
| Violet Evergarden 20 s | 0.1675% | 0.0769% | 46% | 0.1081% | 0.0271% | fp16 |

On Chainsaw the gate's own reference alone reads 0.191% against a 0.1% bar: the refusal survives a
renderer with no quantisation error at all.

## The estimator, not the material: three samples of the same two clips

Each `frames` value samples different times, so these are three populations, not one growing sample.

| clip | sample | test pairs | d2 pairs | d2 int8 | d2 fp16 | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chainsaw | 16 | 8 | 2 | 0.2177% | 0.1910% | fp16 |
| Chainsaw | 24 | 12 | 4 | 0.1603% | 0.1389% | fp16 |
| Chainsaw | 32 | 16 | 5 | 0.1748% | 0.1580% | fp16 |
| Violet | 16 | 8 | 6 | 0.1675% | 0.0769% | fp16 |
| Violet | 24 | 12 | 7 | 0.0382% | 0.0131% | **int8** |
| Violet | 32 | 16 | 11 | 0.0832% | 0.0045% | **int8** |

1. **Chainsaw's refusal is robust and it is the gate's.** The fp16 reference is above the bar in all
   three samples (0.1910 / 0.1389 / 0.1580), and the quantiser contributes 0.017-0.027 pp.
2. **Violet's refusal exists only on the 8-pair sample.** At 12 and 16 pairs the same probe passes, at
   0.0382% and 0.0832%. And with the corrected reference the quantiser owns 95% of Violet's depth-2
   number at 16 pairs (0.0045 of 0.0832 is the reference) - the opposite ownership to Chainsaw.
3. **The registry's suspicion was wrong in an instructive way.** `int8-composition-d2` asked whether one
   of the two numbers was "not the population it is labelled with". Both are; what differs between the
   titles is the *reference term's share*, 88% on Chainsaw against 5-46% on Violet.
4. **The depth-2 statistic is thinner than the engine statistic.** It is the intersection of the test
   pairs with the pairs the gate would reuse: 2 pairs on Chainsaw at the product's sample against 8 for
   the engine statistic and the 13-19 the protocol asks.

## What this does not cover

- The probe approximates the gate's served reference ("both block-mean steps pass") rather than
  modelling the chain state and the pixel veto (E25), so the control measures the quantiser's share of
  the *probe's* depth-2 number, not of the gate's actual reuse.
- The pair order is mirrored: `_read_pairs` returns three frames in time order and the probe renders
  index 0 against references 1 and 2, so the render is the EARLIEST frame of the triple while the gate
  only reuses past frames.
- The fp16 reference is the torch fp16 model, not the TensorRT plan the app falls back to.
