# The gate's threshold does separate - except on the one frame that decides

Measured 2026-11-17 on the 100 frames the shipped gate reused on Chainsaw Man 20 s: the product's own
`ReferenceFrameGate` fed the LR frames (its counters reproduce the render: 100 reused, 2 scene cuts), and
the per-frame HR acceptance share of the gated-vs-ungated decode over all 481 frames. Joining the two
answers the question "does anything the gate can see predict the damage".

| feature | AUC | Spearman vs HR share |
|---|---|---|
| LR share of pixels above 4 levels | **0.993** | +0.66 |
| the gate's own delta | **0.989** | +0.56 |
| LR share above 6 / above 8 | 0.945 / 0.947 | +0.64 / +0.63 |
| LR max \|delta\| (what the veto sees) | 0.910 | +0.49 |
| chain depth | **0.507** | +0.09 |

So the gate's variable is not blind: it ranks the damage almost perfectly on average, and the chain
depth carries no information at all. But the ranking fails on the frame that matters.

## Two classes, and only one of them is visible to any LR statistic

| | frame 164 (worst by share) | frame 432 (worst by peak) |
|---|---|---|
| HR share | **0.4937%** | 0.3053% |
| HR peak | 14 | **75** |
| gate delta | 3.294 | 3.935 |
| chain | 3 | 2 |
| LR share above 8 | **0.000%** | 0.259% |
| LR max \|delta\| | **5.25** | 56.75 |
| bad pixels | 40951 in 63 blocks, 8-16 levels only | 25319 over 379 blocks, up to 75 |
| reference luminance at those pixels | **median 252, 100% above 240** | median 50, p10 24, p90 107 |

Frame 164 is a **saturated white region shifted by 8-14 levels**: 63 blocks fully covered, no pixel
above 16, and every one of them at 252/255 in the ungated render. Frame 432 is the **moving-edge**
class: thin structures, sparse, up to 75 levels, at ordinary exposure.

The gate cannot see 164 at all - its LR max delta is 5.25 and not one LR pixel moves by more than 8. The
error is manufactured by the upscaler from a sub-threshold, spatially smooth change. Seventeen of the
eighteen bad frames are the other class, and they carry LR peaks of 41-75.

## What a threshold would cost, and what the veto would catch

Lowering the gate's delta from 4.0:

| threshold | reuse kept | bad caught |
|---|---|---|
| 3.9 | 97% | 15/18 |
| 3.7 | 95% | 13/18 |
| 3.3 | 90% | 9/18 |
| 3.0 | 84% | 3/18 |

The three frames with the highest delta are not the three worst frames: 164 has a middling 3.294, so
catching it needs 3.3, which still leaves 9 bad frames and costs 10% of the reuse.

The pixel veto is the sharper instrument and it is **off** in the shipped auto path (`pixel veto=off`).
At 40 levels on the LR max delta it keeps 82 of 100 reuses and catches **16 of 18** - it is aimed at
exactly the class that dominates this clip, and it is disabled.

## The probe's reference is not the fp16 model

`_calibrate` loads int8-rounded weights into `model`, and the probe computes `ref` after that call, so
the "fp16" side of every composition number is fp16 arithmetic on quantised weights. Measured on one
frame: peak 4 levels, 0.00000% of pixels above 8, **not bit-identical**. The magnitude is below the bar
and does not move any recorded number, but the reference is not what it is labelled, and the fix is to
compute `ref` before `_calibrate`.
