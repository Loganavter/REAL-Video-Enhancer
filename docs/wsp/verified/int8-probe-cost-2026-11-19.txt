# What the int8 probe costs, split by job: 62.7 s with a certificate, 29.3 s without

Measured 2026-11-18, on BD Violet (`bd_violet20s.mkv`, 37.715 Mbps stream copy), 1920x1080, two rounds per
mode, **one fresh process per run** (a fresh interpreter, CUDA context and model load is what a render
actually pays), `flock -n /var/tmp/opencode/gpu.lock` around every run.

Why the split: the probe does two jobs. It decodes a spread of frames and measures per-layer scales so the
int8 body can run on this video at all (calibration, mandatory), and it compares int8 against fp16 on the
same frames to certify the picture (optional, and its pair set moved with the configuration it certified -
`rve-int8-probe-population-2026-11-18`). Only the second job is now opt-in, so its price has to be on the
record rather than in a reader's head.

## The two modes

| mode | rounds | statistic | cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| calibration only (decode + scales, 24 calibration frames) | 29.301 / 28.869 s | max | **29.30 s** |
| calibration + certificate (reference forwards + quantised twin) | 62.669 / 62.517 s | max | **62.67 s** |
| the certificate's own share | difference | derived | **33.37 s (53% of the probe)** |

Max, not median: the question is what a render pays at worst, and with n=2 a median is a midpoint.

**Caveat on the 33.37 s: it is an upper bound on what the product saves.** This measurement let the probe
build its fp16 reference in torch (its default). The shipped path passes the app's TensorRT engine as the
reference, and TensorRT is faster than torch fp16 on the same frame - so in the product the certificate is
somewhere below 33.37 s, and the calibration-only cost is the same 29.30 s in both cases.

## What that does to the break-even

The saving is from `int8-wall-ab.txt` (the same session, medians of rounds 2-3): 7.12 ms per frame on
Violet, 5.89 on Chainsaw, at 23.976 fps input.

| clip | saving per second of video | break-even, certificate on | break-even, calibration only |
|---|---|---|---|
| Violet BD | 170.7 ms | 6.12 min | **2.86 min** |
| Chainsaw same-shot | 141.2 ms | 7.40 min | **3.46 min** |

So on a 20 s test cut the probe is a net loss either way; on a 24-minute episode it is ~4 minutes saved
minus the probe, i.e. ~3.5 minutes with the certificate and ~4 minutes without. The change does not make
int8 profitable on short clips - it halves what the startup costs and it stops paying 33 s for a verdict
nothing reads.

## What changed

- `backend/src/utils/Int8Probe.py`: `probe(..., certify=True)`. With `certify=False` the module decodes the
  frames, measures the scales, returns them, and does nothing else; `Int8ProbeResult.certified` records the
  difference, and `stats()` prints "calibration only" instead of a percentage - a withheld number must not
  come back as a figure. The certificate code stays: it is how the population defect above was found.
- `backend/src/RenderVideo.py`: a forced `int8Fit` (which is what the Fast preset sets) asks for calibration
  only; `auto` still certifies. The message that said "fp16 engine runs" now fires only when the render is
  actually tiled (`_int8_body_serves_this_render()`), and the "the probe said X" line is suppressed when no
  verdict was taken.
- `backend/tests/test_int8_probe_modes.py`: pins the seam (no percentage in calibration-only output, the
  `certify` default, the tiled/whole-frame test). Suite: 403 passed, 1 failed - `test_db_build_and_queries`,
  which fails on a missing PyYAML in this interpreter and failed identically before the change.

Not covered: one clip, one model, one card; the certificate's cost with the product's TRT reference is not
measured (the direction is stated, the magnitude is not); a 3rd round would tighten the max by a second or
two and is not worth a GPU job.

## Reproduce

```bash
PYTHONPATH=scripts <install-python> scripts/probe_cost.py --rounds 2      # runs the probes
PYTHONPATH=scripts <install-python> scripts/probe_cost.py --from-log      # records from the same log
```

Run: PYTHONPATH=scripts python3 -m harness.cli check docs/wsp/verified/int8-probe-cost-2026-11-19.txt \
       --records docs/wsp/verified/records/int8-probe-cost.jsonl --bars docs/claims-bars.json
