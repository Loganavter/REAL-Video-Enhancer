# The luma cell, measured: the flip does introduce the class

The comparison the flip decision needed is between the two gates on the same clip, same comparator,
same ungated reference - not against an anchor from another population.

| Violet 20 s | worst share | worst block mean (64x64) | at 32x32 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **luma 4.0** (the default) | 0.07345% (f227) | **3.78** (f20) | 5.32 (f22) |
| **channel-max 0.07%** | 0.07345% (f227) | **37.27** (f330) | 60.88 (f342) |

Both gates: zero frames above 0.1%. The block-mean maxima sit on **different frames** and differ by
**7-10x at either block size**, so the hypothesis that the 37.27 frame is reused by both gates is
refuted: the channel-max gate introduces the class on Violet and the luma gate does not have it.

That is what the hold was waiting for, and it is now measured on the cell that decides rather than
against an external anchor.
