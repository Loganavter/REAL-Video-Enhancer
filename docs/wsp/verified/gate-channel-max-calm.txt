# The channel-max gate fixes the failing clip and eats the margin on a calm one

Two verifications that the previous round left open: the same gate on the calm 8 s cut, and whether the
auto tuner can be combined with a share statistic.

## The calm cut: worst 0.08769%, peak 116

| gate | worst frame | peak | above 0.1% | reuse |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| luma 4.0 (shipped) | 0.00274% | 16 | 0 | 66.3% |
| **channel-max 0.07%** | **0.08769%** | **116** | 0 | 66.7% |

Both pass the bar, and the new gate is 32x worse on this clip: it accepts a frame on which a few pixels
moved by up to 116 levels, because the *area* stays under 0.07%. That is the trade the block-max used to
prevent - a small object moving far is a small area - and the share statistic is blind to it by
construction. The margin collapses from 36x to 1.14x on material that was never in trouble.

So the channel-max share is not a drop-in replacement: it fixes the highlight class and gives up the
small-area/large-delta class. The two statistics are complementary, and the gate needs both - a share to
catch the broad shallow case and a block-max to catch the narrow deep one. The block-max alone does not
separate (AUC 0.992), but as a second condition it is exactly what this frame would trip.

## The tuner fails open with a share statistic, and now refuses

With `--temporal_reuse_statistic channel_max_share` and the auto tuner left ON, the tuner wrote its
choice - a threshold in 8-bit levels - into the field the gate now reads as a percentage:

```
CLI:      --temporal_reuse_threshold 0.07 --temporal_reuse_statistic channel_max_share
tuner:    auto gate: threshold 3.0/255, chain 3 ...
result:   reference reuse: 89/122 frames reused (73.0%), threshold=3.0/255
```

3.0 read as "3% of pixels" is never exceeded on this material, so reuse climbs and the defect returns.
The combination is now refused with a loud log and the tuner disabled; the same command then reuses
78/122 (63.9%) at the intended 0.07.
