# The channel-max gate on the two calm cuts: 10.7x on Chainsaw, nothing on Violet

The second title was the open check, and running it exposed a confound in the first one.

| cut | gate | worst | peak | reuse |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chainsaw 8 s | luma 4.0 | 0.00274% (182) | 16 | 66.3% |
| Chainsaw 8 s | **channel-max 0.07%, chain 2** | **0.02932% (182)** | **22** | 66.3% |
| Violet 8 s | luma 4.0 | 0.04989% (230) | 18 | 64.0% |
| Violet 8 s | **channel-max 0.07%, chain 2** | **0.04989% (230)** | **18** | 64.0% |

**Violet is unchanged**: the same worst frame, the same peak, and 235 of 250 frames byte-identical
between the two renders. On that material the two statistics make the same decisions.

**Chainsaw is 10.7x worse** at the same reuse: same worst frame (182), peak 16 -> 22, margin to the bar
from 36x to 3.4x. Both still pass.

## The confound, and what it would have cost

The first run of the Chainsaw cut used **chain 3** while its luma baseline ran **chain 2**, and it read
0.08769% at peak **116**. That number was an artefact of the unmatched chain: 0 of 249 frames were
identical between the two renders, which is the signature of a different reuse set rather than a
different statistic. Matched at chain 2 the effect is 0.02932% at peak 22, and every frame still differs
because the two gates pick different references at the same reuse count.

So the calm-cut regression is real but an order of magnitude smaller than the unmatched comparison
suggested, and it is content-specific: present on Chainsaw, absent on Violet.

## The tuner fails open with a share statistic, and now refuses

```
CLI:      --temporal_reuse_threshold 0.07 --temporal_reuse_statistic channel_max_share
tuner:    auto gate: threshold 3.0/255, chain 3 ...
result:   reference reuse: 89/122 frames reused (73.0%), threshold=3.0/255
```

3.0 read as "3% of pixels" is never exceeded on this material, so reuse climbs and the defect returns.
The combination is now refused with a loud log and the tuner disabled; the same command then reuses
78/122 (63.9%) at the intended 0.07.
