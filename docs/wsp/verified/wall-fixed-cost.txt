## The wall: 1.118x, replacing the retracted 1.55x

`--output null` routes the write through rawvideo and the null muxer, so the encoder's dependence on
content entropy is out of the loop and the wall is read + gate + inference.

| configuration | render loop |
|---|---|
| int8 body | **45.02 ms/frame** |
| fp16 engine | **50.33 ms/frame** |
| ratio | **1.118x** |

The retracted pair read 1.55x because ffv1 encodes a quantised picture faster; at a fixed output cost
the wall ratio is 1.118, below the 1.49-1.505 engine ratio, as Amdahl requires when the non-engine term
is not zero.

## The depth table, from the render rather than a replay

`RVE_DEBUG_DEPTH=1` logs every reuse with its depth, and the gate prints the histogram:

```
reference reuse: 83/481 frames reused (17.3%), max chain=3; depth histogram {1: 41, 2: 36, 3: 6}
d1: n=41  worst HR 0.07546%   (frame 475, the clip's worst)
d2: n=36  worst HR 0.02670%
d3: n=6   worst HR 0.00917%
```

So the earlier statement survives on the correct population: the worst frame of the clip is a **depth-1
reuse**, and depth 3 is eight times milder. The replay's table (78 reuses, 38/34/6) is superseded.

## The reference: torch fp16 against the app's TRT fp16

The probe measures against the torch model while the app falls back to a TensorRT plan. On one frame the
two differ by **0.0116%** of pixels above 8 levels, peak 15.1 - an order of magnitude below the bar, and
not the cause of the 4.8% gap between the probe's control and the full sweep. That gap is the pair set:
the probe re-decides the gate on its own frames instead of replaying the render's decisions.
