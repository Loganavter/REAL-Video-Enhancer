# The head is one kernel now: 5.3 ms of the frame, 0.00018% of pixels

Measured 2026-11-20 (local), RTX 4060 Ti, BD Violet 1920x1080, int8 body. The head is 3 -> 64 3x3 fp16, and
K = 27 means the whole 3x3x3 window of a pixel is ONE dot padded to 32 - not nine taps.
Records: `int8-head-fusion.jsonl`; raw: `logs/head-fusion/probe.json`, `logs/head-fusion/ab/`,
`logs/head-fusion/pair.json`.

Row `rve-head-quantise-fusion-2026-11-19` (researcher's C2). The shipped head is three steps: a cuDNN fp16
convolution (4.65-4.90 ms in the app), a separate PReLU, and a quantise pass that reads the 265 MB fp16
activation and writes 133 MB of codes (1.5-1.6 ms). Its arithmetic intensity is 26 FLOP/byte - 3.3x its own
traffic floor - so no layout knob can save it: what it writes is the target, and fusing the epilogue is how
you stop writing it.

## Isolated, on one frame

| arm | median of rounds 2+ |
|---|---|
| head + PReLU + quantise as shipped | **5.658 ms** |
| cuDNN head conv alone | 3.813 ms |
| head conv + quantise pass | 5.577 ms |
| **one kernel: conv + bias + PReLU + requant** | **0.662 ms** |

**-5.0 ms, 88% of the head block.** The fused kernel is also 5.8x the cuDNN convolution alone, which is what
a shape with 26 FLOP/byte should look like once it stops writing 265 MB it immediately re-reads: the kernel
moves ~189 MB (read the 3 planes through nine shifted views, write 133 MB of codes) and 0.66 ms is its
traffic floor at 288 GB/s, so it is at the floor rather than near it.

Codes: **0.102% differ by 1, never more** - the accumulation order over the 27 terms is not cuDNN's, so this
is a different-arithmetic change and the picture is what decides it.

## In the app

Null sink, one session, arms alternating, 3 rounds:

| round | head shipped | head fused |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 44.55 | 38.99 |
| 2 | 44.45 | 39.18 |
| 3 | 44.43 | 39.34 |
| **median** | **44.45** | **39.18** |

**-5.27 ms per frame (-11.9%)**, 3 rounds of 3. The phase counter attributes it: head + quantise reads
**6.20 -> 0.70 ms** (the HEAD line alone 4.90 -> 0.85, the QUANTISE line 1.54 -> 0.01), with the hops and the
tail unchanged.

One thing the app made clear and the probe could not: the shipped model's head is widened to **8** input
channels by `PadInputConv` (the engine's fusion trick, zero-padded), so the kernel takes the first three input
channels of that weight - the zeros contribute nothing, which is why the padded and plain heads compute the
same function.

## Adopted

`RVE_HEAD_FUSED` defaults to **1** since 2026-11-20; `RVE_HEAD_FUSED=0` restores cuDNN + quantise. The row's
own falsifier ("if it does not beat 6.25 ms by 1.5 ms, the shape is at its floor") is answered the other way:
it beats it by 5.3.

Guard: the fused path needs a plain 3-channel (or zero-padded wider) head convolution with `feat` outputs; a
model that has anything else in `body[0]` is served by the shipped path. The quantise-alone diagnostic is
skipped when the head is fused, because there is no separate pass left to time.

## Not covered

- One card, one clip, one label. The head's 3 -> 64 is this model family's shape.
- The picture is a config-vs-config pair plus two model-referenced frames, not 494 frames against the model.
- The 26 FLOP/byte argument is from `int8-head-conv.txt`; this file does not re-derive it.
- Timing is null sink, reuse off; the NVENC arm is not measured here, and the ffv1 renders read ~58.6 ms/frame
  in both arms because the encoder, not the model, owns that loop.
- Suite: `pytest backend/tests` reads 409 passed, 1 failed - `ModuleNotFoundError: yaml` in
  `harness/db.py` under the app's portable interpreter. No file of this change is on that path.
