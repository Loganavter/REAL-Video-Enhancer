# On real action the gate refuses itself - so "action" is not what breaks it

Two cuts at the timestamps the owner named, both 20 s, both `-c copy` so the source noise is untouched:
Chainsaw Man ep01 from 19:40 (the start of the battle) and Violet Evergarden ep01 from 14:23. Rendered
with the shipped defaults against `--no_temporal_reuse`.

| cut | noise floor | gate | full sweep |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chainsaw Man 19:40 | **22.58** | **reuse OFF** - above the 1.5 limit | **0.00000%** over 503 frames |
| Violet Evergarden 14:23 | **36.73** | **reuse OFF** - above the 1.5 limit | **0.00000%** over 487 frames |

The tuner's own words: *"noise floor 22.58 is above the 1.5 limit for this material, so reuse is OFF - at
this noise the measured worst frame is 0.105-0.470% of pixels above 8 levels, past the 0.1% acceptance
bar."* With reuse off the gated render is the ungated render, and the sweep confirms it frame for frame.

So the 20 s clip that failed is not "the gate cannot handle action". It is the opposite: on noisy action
the gate protects the picture by not engaging at all. The clip that failed has a noise floor of **0.66** -
clean enough to engage - and 20.8% reuse, with 17 of its 18 bad frames in a low-amplitude motion
sequence. The trigger is *clean material with motion the block mean does not see*, not motion.

## The comparator, and what it cost

The sweep used to run the 8.3-megapixel comparison in numpy: 0.19 s per frame, 234 s for 481 frames.
Moved to the card (torch.cuda, decode still on the CPU), the same sweep takes **55 s** and produces
**bit-identical** per-frame shares and peaks - 481 of 481, 18 above the bar, worst 0.49372% on frame 164.
The decode does not benefit: ffv1 has no GPU decoder, and `-hwaccel cuda` measured 26.9 s against 24.0 s
for the same file.

One process note worth keeping: `/tmp` is a 20 GiB tmpfs and the renders filled it, truncating one
`--no_temporal_reuse` render at 338 of 487 frames. Counting frames before the sweep caught it; a sweep
that trusted the file would have reported 0.00000% over a partial clip. Count frames before any sweep.
