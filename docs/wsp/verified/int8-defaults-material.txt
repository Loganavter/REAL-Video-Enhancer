# Both adopted changes hold on other material: head -4.4 to -5.6 ms, tail -3.1 to -3.5 ms

Measured 2026-11-20 (local), RTX 4060 Ti, three clips, each change reverted in its own interleaved A/B, null
sink, reuse off, 3 rounds per clip, medians of rounds 2-3. Per clip, never pooled: the clips are different
populations and the row was filed because both adoptions were measured on **one** clip.
Records: `int8-defaults-material.jsonl`; logs: `logs/second-material/*-{head,tail}/`.

Row `rve-int8-defaults-second-material-2026-11-20`. The two changes adopted earlier the same day
(`int8-head-fusion.txt`: -5.27 ms on Violet; `int8-tail-hop.txt`: -3.16 ms on Violet) were measured on BD
Violet only. This is the replay on two other clips, one of which is new material made for the purpose.

## The material

| clip | frames | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| `bd_violet20s.mkv` | 494 | BD Violet, 1920x1080, 8-bit h264, the clip both adoptions were measured on |
| `bd_csm_same.mkv` | 539 | Chainsaw Man same-shot cut, 1920x1080, 8-bit h264 |
| `native1440_20s.mkv` | 495 | **native** 1440x1080 10-bit hevc, SAR 1:1 (a real 4:3 picture), cut 2026-11-19 from `Untitled78.mov` at 36.036 s |
| `violet1440.mkv` | 494 | 1440x1080 but ANANAMORPHIC: a 4:3 squeeze of the BD cut, i.e. a 16:9 picture in 1440 columns - same pixel count, different shape |

## The head as one fused kernel

| clip | cuDNN head + quantise | fused | delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| BD Violet | (45.87-era baseline) | - | **-5.27** |
| Chainsaw | 44.02 | 38.38 | **-5.64** |
| native 1440x1080 | 33.46 | 29.04 | **-4.42** |
| violet1440 (anamorphic) | 33.05 | 28.20 | -4.85 |

**-4.4 to -5.6 ms on three clips of two resolutions and two bit depths**, i.e. 13-15% of the frame each time.
The gain is a property of the path (one kernel instead of conv + PReLU + quantise), as the isolated probe
said it would be, and it does not depend on the clip's content.

## The tail as one int8 kernel with the shuffle folded in

| clip | dequant + cuDNN tail + shuffle | int8 tail + fold | delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| BD Violet | (45.87-era baseline) | - | **-3.16** |
| Chainsaw | 38.02 | 34.56 | **-3.46** |
| native 1440x1080 | 31.87 | 28.79 | **-3.08** |

**-3.1 to -3.5 ms on three clips.** Note what the arm reverted here is the WHOLE tail change (the int8
convolution, the removed dequant pass, and the shuffle fold adopted later the same day), because
`RVE_TAIL_INT8=0` also disables the fold's precondition - so this is the end-to-end tail gain, not one of its
parts.

## One draw worth naming

`bd_csm_same_tail0_r1` reads **42.98** against 38.02 for rounds 2-3 of the same arm - a 13% outlier in the
first round only, which is exactly what the "rounds 2-3" rule exists for. It is recorded in the draws, not
smoothed: every number above is the median of two draws, and the two agree to 0.01-0.02 ms in every arm.

## What this closes

The row's own refutation was "the gains are properties of the path and hold on other material". They do, on
two resolutions, two bit depths, two titles and a 4:3 frame. What changes is only their size, by ~20%
between clips (the head is -4.4 on 1440 and -5.6 on Chainsaw), which is the kind of spread that makes a
pooled number wrong and a per-clip number right.

## Not covered

- The picture on these clips is NOT re-measured here: the acceptance pairs for both changes are on BD Violet
  (`int8-head-fusion.txt`, `int8-tail-epilogue.txt`). A 4:3 10-bit clip has its own colour path and its own
  record (`int8-1440x1080.txt`), and mixing them would be the population error this row exists to avoid.
- One card, null sink, gate off.
- `violet1440.mkv` is anamorphic - it exercises the same 1440x1080 pixel count and therefore the same GPU
  work, but its picture is a horizontally squeezed 16:9 frame. It is kept in the table as a second draw on
  the same geometry, not as a second kind of material.

## Reproduce

```bash
for clip in native1440_20s.mkv bd_csm_same.mkv; do
  d=docs/wsp/verified/logs/second-material/${clip%.mkv}
  ARMS="head0 head1" ARM_ENV_HEAD0="RVE_HEAD_FUSED=0" ARM_ENV_HEAD1="RVE_HEAD_FUSED=1" \
    CLIPS="$clip" ROUNDS=3 SINK=null LOGDIR="$d-head" scripts/wall_ab.sh
  ARMS="tail0 tail1" ARM_ENV_TAIL0="RVE_TAIL_INT8=0" ARM_ENV_TAIL1="RVE_TAIL_INT8=1" \
    CLIPS="$clip" ROUNDS=3 SINK=null LOGDIR="$d-tail" scripts/wall_ab.sh
done
PYTHONPATH=. python3 harness/scripts/second_material_records.py
```
