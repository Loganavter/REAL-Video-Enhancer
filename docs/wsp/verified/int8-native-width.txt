# Native widths: 32 is free, 48 costs what 64 costs, and 24 is cheap because it is shallow

Measured 2026-11-20 (local), records `int8-width.jsonl` (WX001-WX020, generated below), probe `harness/scripts/int8_width_probe.py`,
raw `logs/int8-width/probe.json`. Ten models from the collection that the pipeline refused before, at their own
widths: five Archivist denoisers (48 channels), three RGB-max-Denoise (32), two SuperUltraCompact (24).

## The finding that shaped this: the width is not free

```
tl.arange(0, 48)  ->  "arange's range must be a power of 2"
tl.dot (int8)     ->  K must be a multiple of 16
```

48 satisfies the second (3x16) and not the first, so a 48-channel body does not compile at all in Triton - the
first run died with exactly that message. The body now pads to the next power of two (48 -> 64, 24 -> 32) with
zero weights and neutral scales: the padding channels carry 0 through every hop and the tail reads only the first
`cout`. A power-of-two width pays nothing.

## Measured

| width | models | share (worst of 3 frames) | body, ms | what the padding costs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **32**, native | 3x RGB-max-Denoise | 0.0000% | **11.80 / 11.81 / 11.80** | nothing |
| **24 -> 32**, padded | 2x SuperUltraCompact | 0.0000% | **6.22 / 6.30** | the model is also shallow: 8 inner convs |
| **48 -> 64**, padded | 5x Archivist | 0.0000-0.0004% | **27.14-27.32** | 1.33x work: it costs exactly what a 64-channel body costs |

**And the demand is not there for a 48-channel kernel.** The owner's survey of a ~500-model corpus found nine
with 48 channels; this collection holds five, all 1x denoisers. So the CUDA-with-three-16-wide-tiles route is
closed by population rather than by difficulty - the padding path already runs them at 64-channel speed, and
nobody is waiting for the 25% it would give back.

The 48 row is the honest answer to "is 48 cheaper than 64": **no**. The hop runs at 64 channels whatever the model
has, so the 44% of arithmetic a 48-channel model does not need is paid anyway. Getting 48 to pay for 48 needs
three 16-wide mma tiles, which Triton cannot express - that is a CUDA kernel, and it is a width-generality
question, not an optimisation (the same kernel measured flat on every optimisation lever, `int8-hop-tapreuse.txt`).

The 24 row shows the other half of the scaling: at the same padded width (32), eight inner convs cost about half
of sixteen (6.2 against 11.8 ms), so depth and width scale roughly independently.

## The tile was not mis-tuned

`BLOCK_M=128, warps=4` is the shipped default, tuned at 64 channels. At a padded 48 it is also the best of the
nine combinations tried:

```
BLOCK_M  64 warps 2  29.76   BLOCK_M 128 warps 2  28.17   BLOCK_M 256 warps 2  75.31
BLOCK_M  64 warps 4  38.61   BLOCK_M 128 warps 4  27.25   BLOCK_M 256 warps 4  28.46
BLOCK_M  64 warps 8  41.84   BLOCK_M 128 warps 8  39.39   BLOCK_M 256 warps 8  29.97
```

So the re-tuning this row was written for turns out to be a no-op - worth having measured rather than assumed,
because 256x2 is 2.8x slower and a default picked at another width could have landed there.

## What this does not say

- **The shares are three-frame smoke tests.** None of these ten models has a 494-frame pair, and the 1% start-up
  gate is not the 0.1% picture bar. The ranking inside this page is trustworthy; the verdicts are not.
- **The ratio is not measured, and it is the number that matters for the lane.** A cheaper model has a cheaper
  fp16 comparator too - measured on the eight-hop model, the frame fell 12.7 ms while the ratio moved 1.6648 ->
  1.6965. For a 48-channel denoiser at 27.2 ms of body the ratio is likely *worse* than the production model's
  1.665, because its engine is faster while its body is not.
- **These ten are not the product.** Five are 1x denoisers, two are 2x "SuperUltraCompact" restore models. The
  value of the page is coverage - the pipeline no longer refuses them - not throughput.
- **Padding needs the two int8 boundaries** (fused head, int8 tail): the two fp16 passes are not padded, and the
  body refuses a padded width without them instead of writing a buffer of the wrong width.

flock -n /var/tmp/opencode/gpu.lock <install-python> harness/scripts/int8_width_probe.py \
    --models "Archivist_AntiLines.pth,1x-RGB-max-Denoise.pth,2x-DigitalFlim-SuperUltraCompact.pth" --sweep \
    --out-file docs/wsp/verified/logs/int8-width/probe.json
PYTHONPATH=. python3 harness/harness/scripts/int8_width_records.py
