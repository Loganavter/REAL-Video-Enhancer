# Per-hop K: 2.3-4x better, on a quantity that is already three orders inside the bar

Measured 2026-11-20, crops calibration, three held-out frames, `Int8Body` with `RVE_INT8_GRID_K` as a per-hop list
(new: `"2,3,2,..."`). Records `int8-grid-k-perlayer.jsonl` (PK001-PK004), raw
`logs/grid-perlayer-{futsuu,realesr}.json`, probe `scripts/grid_k_perlayer_probe.py`.

This is stage (b) of `rve-grid-k-learned-2026-11-20` - PAMS's falsifier, done without training. PAMS says a
**learned** per-layer truncated scale beats a fixed one. If the per-layer choice made by *search* on the same frames
cannot beat the best single K, a learned one will not either, and the training loop never gets written.

## The two models, chosen at the ends of the ladder

| model | best single K | per-hop greedy K | gain |
|---|---|---|---|
| `2x-Futsuu-Anime(1)` | K=2, **0.000106%** | **0.000027%** | 3.96x |
| `realesr-animevideov3(1)` | K=3, **0.003283%** | **0.001415%** | 2.32x |

Futsuu is the model the lane ships against (K=2 already clean); realesr is the ladder's second-worst case at K=2
(0.05038%), so it is the model where a per-layer choice had the most room to help.

## What that says

- **Per-hop K does help, and by a real factor** - 2.3x on the model with the most room, 4x on the clean one. The
  mechanism is what the ladder already showed per model, now per hop: different hops want different floors.
- **The absolute numbers are negligible.** 0.000106% -> 0.000027% is three orders below the 0.1% bar; 0.0033% ->
  0.0014% is 70x below it. The quantity the training loop would optimise is already invisible to the decision
  statistic.
- **So stage (c) is not justified.** A learned scale (PAMS-style, fine-tuning the scale with weights frozen) would
  chase the same 2-4x on the same negligible quantity, at the cost of a training loop, a new artefact to version,
  and a start-up that depends on training convergence instead of a 3 s sweep.

## Verdict

Row `rve-grid-k-learned-2026-11-20` closes: the offline per-hop search shows the per-layer idea is real but the
prize is three orders below the bar, so the learned variant is not worth a kernel, a training loop, or a start-up
dependency. The per-hop list stays available (`RVE_INT8_GRID_K="2,3,..."`) as a probe, off by default - the shipped
path keeps the single-K sweep with its K=2-clean skip.

## What is NOT claimed

- **Two models, three frames.** The greedy search is per model and per frame set; a different clip could move the
  per-hop optimum.
- **Greedy, not global.** The per-hop vector is found one hop at a time from the best single K, so the reported gain
  is a lower bound on what a full search would find - which only strengthens the verdict.
- **No training was run.** That is the point: stage (b) was designed to decide whether to write it.
