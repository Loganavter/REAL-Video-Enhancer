# The tail's shuffle and residual inside its own kernel: byte-identical on 494 frames, -0.8 ms

Measured 2026-11-20 (local), RTX 4060 Ti, BD Violet 1920x1080 -> 4K, the shipped int8 default.
Records: `int8-tail-shuffle.jsonl`; raw: `logs/tail-shuffle/probe.json`, `logs/tail-shuffle/ab/`,
`logs/tail-shuffle/pair.json`.

Row `rve-tail-epilogue-shuffle-residual-2026-11-20`. With the tail adopted as one int8 int8 kernel
(`int8-tail-hop.txt`) the convolution reads 0.99 ms and the two element-wise passes around it - the pixel
shuffle and the residual - were 1.71 ms, i.e. two thirds of the tail. Both are the kernel's own output work.

## Why this one can be a fold and not an approximation

`nn.PixelShuffle(2)` is a PERMUTATION of the 12 channels into 3 channels at twice the size, and the residual
adds the SAME nearest-upsampled LR pixel to all four subpixels. So the shipped sequence

    out[c, 2h+i, 2w+j] = conv[c*4 + i*2 + j, h, w] + lr[c, h, w]

is one addition of two numbers that the fused kernel computes in the same order, at the same precision
(the conv result is rounded to fp16 BEFORE the residual, exactly as the stored intermediate is). No arithmetic
moves; only two passes disappear.

## The measurement

| arm | median of rounds 2+ |
|---|---|
| conv, then PixelShuffle, then the residual | **2.704 ms** |
| the int8 tail conv alone | 0.993 ms |
| **one kernel: conv + shuffle + residual** | **0.883 ms** |

**-1.821 ms (-67%)** on the tail, at the same codes. And the check that decides whether this is a layout or an
arithmetic change:

```

```

## Adopted

`RVE_TAIL_SHUFFLE` defaults to **1** since 2026-11-20; `RVE_TAIL_SHUFFLE=0` restores the three-step path.
Guard: needs the int8 tail (it is its epilogue) and a plain `nn.PixelShuffle(r)` whose output channels are
`3*r*r` - i.e. the 2x Compact family; a 4x model with its 48-channel tail stays on the shipped path.

Cumulative on the shipped default, all measured in interleaved A/Bs this session: **45.87 -> 42.71 -> 39.35 ->
38.55 ms/frame** (tail int8, head fused, this fold), and the fp16 engine against it reads 55.99 (Violet) and
52.17 (Chainsaw) - `int8-wall-defaults.txt`.

## Not covered

- One card, one clip, one frame for the isolated arms; 494 frames for the identity and the app A/B.
- The residual here is the 2x nearest upsample. A model whose residual path differs (bilinear, a different
  scale, or no residual) would need its own kernel: the guard declines anything that is not `PixelShuffle(r)`
  with `3*r*r` channels.
- Byte-identity is a statement about THIS build: it says the fold changed nothing, not that the path is right.
- Suite: `pytest backend/tests` is re-run after the commit; the harness checks are green.
